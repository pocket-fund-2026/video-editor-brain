# Error taxonomy (use these category names in lessons)
Borrowed from the AgentDebug idea: classify every failure so the fix targets the cause, not the symptom.
- brand-fit: palette, fonts or elements that look like another brand or like AI output
- layout-overlap: text colliding, duplicated or cramped
- safe-zone: content under platform buttons or outside the frame
- factual-claim: an unverified price, hour, date, distance or quote on screen
- attribution-license: missing credit or an unlicensed asset
- timing-sync: picture and audio or captions out of step
- audio: levels, voice quality, pronunciation
- tooling-limit: the tool cannot do what was promised
- process-skipped-verification: shipped without rendering or checking
- layout-css: a styling bug (sizing, stacking, overflow)
- map-reuse: map engine, data or zoom conventions
