## Identity
- Name: Cafe Madras (Udupi / South Indian). Opened August 1940; run by the third generation of the Kamath family; recipes passed down (Slurrp, https://slurrp.com/article/cafe-madras-mumbais-favourite-spot-for-timeless-south-indian-flavours-1724847463488). bombaycafemap.com page says "open since 1940" too.
- Location: King's Circle, Matunga East (Slurrp: quiet lane near King Circle). bombaycafemap.com page address: 38 B, Circle House, King's Circle, Near Maheshwari Udyan. OSM node "Cafe Madras" (amenity=restaurant, node 1977933168) at 19.0276726, 72.8550473 on Bhau Daji Road: pin_verified = true (OSM).
