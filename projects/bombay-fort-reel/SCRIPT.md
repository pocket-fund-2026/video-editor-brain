## Narration (about 120 words)

[Hook, 0:00]
You've definitely heard of this cafe. Britannia and Co, Ballard Estate. Serving lunch since 1923.

[History, 0:05]
An Iranian immigrant, Rashid Kohinoor, opened it. His son Boman took over at twenty, and kept it going until he died at ninety seven.

[The dish, 0:14]
And there's one reason people still make the trip. The berry pulao. Saffron rice, chicken or mutton, and tart barberries still brought in from Iran.

[Verdict, 0:24]
So, worth the trip? Yes. Definitely. Order the berry pulao, take your time, and sit inside a hundred years of old Bombay. Public rating, four point two.

[Visit card, 0:35]
Lunch only, closed by four on weekdays, shut on Sundays. Bring cash, and go hungry.
