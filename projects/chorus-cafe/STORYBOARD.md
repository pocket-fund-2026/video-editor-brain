# Chorus Café: animation plan (v1, for discussion)

Concept: **The Stitched Map**. Everything is thread on cloth. Nothing glows, nothing floats for no reason.
Voice: Sana (ElevenLabs web app, generated by hand), foley only: needle pull, cloth rustle, ceramic clink, scissor snip. No music bed.
Length: about 60 s (Sana reads at about 2.1 words a second). Format 1080x1920.

## Look rules (anti-slop, lesson L013)
- Flat colour and woven texture only: no gradients, glows, blur or emoji.
- Stitches are uneven on purpose: spacing and angle wobble seeded from each street's position, so it looks hand-made, not random shimmer.
- A physical reason for every move: a needle pulls the thread; tags hang from a string and swing like a pendulum; patches are pressed on.
- Real specifics only: real Kala Ghoda streets, the real pin, the menu's own words and prices.
- Two typefaces: Fraunces (headlines, italic for emphasis) and DM Mono (tiny labels). Left-weighted, asymmetric layouts.

## Production approach
- A canvas draws stitches as short thick segments with round caps and a slightly darker offset for thickness (no blur). Cloth is a seeded woven-noise texture, drawn once.
- Streets come from OpenStreetMap through `tools/maps/fetch_region.py` (credit "Map data: OpenStreetMap contributors" on screen).
- Tags swing as a damped pendulum, computed from time, so scrubbing and export stay exact.
- Everything is a pure function of time. Checks: palette (no Claude lookalike), overlap and safe zones, facts (only strings in `verified.json`), design variety, and the new anti-slop check.
