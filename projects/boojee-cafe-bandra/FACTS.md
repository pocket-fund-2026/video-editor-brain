- Boojee Cafe, Bandra West. Opened 2019 as a 15-seater on Perry Cross Road; chef-owned by Rajdeep Singh Kukreja (Free Press Journal https://www.freepressjournal.in/lifestyle/from-bandra-to-sobo-boojee-cafe-brings-its-classic-brews-brunch-to-south-mumbai ; Time Out).
- Address on bombaycafemap.com: Shop 6, New Kantwadi Road, off Perry Cross Road, Bandra West. Page: /mumbai/boojee-cafe-perry-road. OSM node 11298556969 (amenity=cafe, "Boojee Café", New Kantwadi Road) at 19.0606201, 72.8244865: pin verified.
- Name meaning: staff told a 2019 reviewer "boojee" means a luxurious lifestyle with humble beginnings (foodhistjourney blog review). Show as "Staff say: ..." not as a dictionary claim.
- Look (2019 review): simple black and white, graffiti walls with quotes, indoor and outdoor seating.
