# Kyani & Co., Marine Lines: sourced facts (no prices, ever: L049)

## On the Bombay Cafe Map page (bombaycafemap.com/mumbai/kyani-co-marine-lines, verified 2026-09-08)
- 1904. Marble tables, slow fans, bun maska. "The oldest room on this map."
- Marble tops, aged wooden chairs, high ceilings, shared tables; seating is limited, you may sit with strangers.
- Jer Mahal Estate, Jagannath Shankar Seth Road, Marine Lines. Listed under Irani cafe, institution, since 1904.
- Not for laptops: no wifi, laptops not allowed. Content angle = Old Bombay institution, food first.
- Do NOT show the page score (0.6/5) or any price.

## Dishes (from outside sources)
- Bun maska, brun maska, mawa cake, sweet milky Irani chai: unchanged for a century (Tablejourney / Outlook Traveller guides, web search 2026-11-13)
- Keema pav: named in reviews and lists (mutton dhansak, kheema pav, chicken farcha for fuller meals)
- Parsi chicken patties and mawa cakes were core items (Parsi Khabar / Culture Trip)
- Handwritten bills; chequered tablecloths, big mirrors, ceiling fans (Culture Trip, LBB)
- NOT confirmed, do not use: Shrewsbury biscuits; brun maska "best in city" (sources credit Yazdani).

## History
- Built 1904; taken over in late 1959 by Aflatoon Shokri; now run by his son Farokh Shokri (Parsi Khabar, Zoroastrians.net). Oldest surviving Irani cafe in the city (Culture Trip).
- Hero food: mawa cake and bun maska. Chai is a side element, never the hero pour (L041).
