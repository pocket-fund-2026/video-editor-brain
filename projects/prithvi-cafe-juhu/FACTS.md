# Prithvi Cafe, Juhu: sourced facts (checked 2026-11-11)
Press-sourced only; no official Prithvi page was checked.
- Prithvi Theatre was founded in Juhu in 1978 by Shashi Kapoor and Jennifer Kendal (Mumbai Live, Wikipedia). On screen: "1978", "Prithvi Theatre founded".
- The cafe began as a basic eatery for drama students during their breaks (Mumbai Live). On screen and in voice: "The cafe began as a basic eatery for drama students on their breaks" / "It began as a drama students' snack stop."
- Dishes named in press: kheema pav (Mumbai Live), Amritsari kulcha (LBB and reviews), Irish coffee (Mumbai Live, LBB). No tea or chai shown.
- NOT shown: prices, hours, distances, ratings, the cafe's own founding year, seating counts.
- Map: OpenStreetMap data for Juhu; Prithvi Theatre at 19.1062109, 72.8256355 (Nominatim). The lime block is the OSM footprint made taller, not a surveyed model.
