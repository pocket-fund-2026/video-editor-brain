# Design moves library (the innovation engine)
Rule: every film changes at least 3 unlocked design dimensions against the last 3 films and tries ONE move it has never used (the experiment). Locked dimensions (a reference video's palette, a client logo) may repeat. `tools/loop/suggest_moves.py` picks fresh moves from this list; add new ones whenever you invent or spot one. Format: `- [dimension] name: description`.

## Moves
- [opening] city-shape zoom: open on the real outline of the city, highlight the area, zoom into the street
- [opening] macro object reveal: start inside a cup or plate close-up and pull back to the venue
- [opening] receipt print-out: a till receipt prints the headline line by line
- [opening] route-line draw: a single line draws across the map and becomes the title underline
- [opening] polaroid stack: photos land one by one with a slight tilt, the last one flips to the title
- [opening] time-lapse day to night: the map lights change from morning to evening behind the headline
- [opening] split-screen then-and-now: two halves slide apart to reveal old and new
- [transition] continuous camera glide: no cuts, the camera travels between scenes on the map
- [transition] paper tear: the next scene is revealed through a torn paper edge
- [transition] shutter iris: a camera-iris closes and reopens on the next scene
- [transition] match cut on shape: a circle in one scene becomes the coin, plate or clock of the next
- [transition] page flip: scenes are pages in a notebook or passport
- [transition] zoom through a letter: the camera dives through the counter of a letter into the next scene
- [camera] orbit and glide: slow orbit around a pin, then a whip to the next
- [camera] top-down grid walk: a flat top-down camera snaps along street blocks
- [camera] dolly-in on facade: push toward a building front until its door fills the frame
- [camera] parallax layers: foreground, midground and background move at different speeds
- [camera] rack focus: blur and sharpen between two foreground items
- [typography] bold sans plus mono labels: heavy headline with tiny mono section labels
- [typography] serif editorial: large serif headlines with italic accents
- [typography] kinetic stack: words stack and resize to fill the frame
- [typography] outline to fill: letters draw as outlines then fill with colour
- [typography] handwritten marks: underline, circle and arrow drawn on by hand
- [typography] variable weight pulse: the headline weight swells on the beat
- [layout] top headline, centre visual, bottom captions: the standard vertical stack
- [layout] boarding pass: facts laid out as a ticket with a barcode strip
- [layout] magazine grid: asymmetrical boxes of image, number and text
- [layout] receipt column: one narrow column of line items down the centre
- [layout] polaroid wall: facts as captions under pinned photos
- [motion_device] rubber-stamp verdict: a stamp lands with a small shake
- [motion_device] count-up meter: a number and bar climb to the value
- [motion_device] ticket card: a swaying ticket with icon rows
- [motion_device] pin drop: a teardrop pin falls, squashes and ripples
- [motion_device] sticker slap: stickers slap on with a squash and a pop
- [motion_device] ingredient lines: typographic list with drawn rules
- [motion_device] polaroid develop: an image fades up from white like a developing photo
- [motion_device] progress ring: a ring fills while a fact is read out
- [texture] 3D extruded map: real building footprints raised in 3D
- [texture] paper grain with halftone: print texture over flat colour
- [texture] blueprint grid: white lines on blue with measurement ticks
- [texture] risograph overprint: two-colour offset print look
- [texture] frosted glass cards: blurred translucent panels over the map
- [sound] marimba pad groove: warm mallet pulse under a soft pad
- [sound] foley only: no music, only real-world sounds timed to the picture
- [sound] tabla and bansuri bed: Indian percussion and flute under the voice
- [sound] lo-fi cafe ambience: cups, murmur and a street bed
- [ending] logo steam pop: the cup logo pops in with curling steam
- [ending] poll card: a question with option pills
- [ending] map pull-out: the camera pulls back to the whole city
- [ending] stamp the passport: the film ends by stamping a passport page
- [ending] next-stop teaser: the end card previews the next location
